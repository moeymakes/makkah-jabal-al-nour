Build a real-time, realistic-but-painterly scene of Jabal al-Nour above Makkah that runs in the browser. It only
needs to look beautiful and let me walk the path (and, with a key, fly) around the mountain.

PRIORITIES (in this order when they conflict)
1. Visual quality
2. Stable frame pacing at the target spec
3. Small bundle size

CONSTRAINTS
- No Three.js/Babylon.js or other engines. WebGPU rendered.
- Everything procedural: no texture/model/audio files. Deliverable is a single self-contained index.html.
  Report minified size.
- Zero per-frame JS allocations (no GC stutter). Preallocate all buffers.
- Sacred site: no human figures, no depiction of the Prophet (peace be upon him), his family, the Companions or any
  person; no animals suggesting a scene with people (a few birds are fine). No interiors: the Cave of Hira is shown
  from outside only. No synthesized adhan, talbiyah or Qur'an recitation; no scripture or any text anywhere, no
  graffiti or signs. Buildings are respectful approximations; geography composed and compressed, not to scale.

TARGET
- Omarchy Linux, Chrome 157, RTX 3070, 60 Hz+ monitor
- 2560x1440 at full resolution. GPU frame time <= 11 ms average during a scripted 60 s fly-through.

SCENE
- The steep granite mountain north-east of the Haram: dark weathered boulders, a stepped path with low walls and
  railings zig-zagging up, rest platforms, the summit ridge
- The small Cave of Hira in a cleft of rocks just below the top, facing toward the Kaaba
- Makkah spread in the valleys below: dense houses and blocks, the Haram and the clock tower visible far off, other
  bare mountains around
- Time setting: Dawn (default), Golden hour, Night (city lights below, stars above)
- Life: a few birds; dust in the low light
- World: bounded, but the boundaries can't be visible from the path

CONTROLS
WASD walk with gravity, mouse look (pointer lock or drag), Shift to run, F toggles flight (E lifts off, Q lands)

VERIFICATION
- Visual critic: a separate subagent that sees ONLY the renders: 6 fixed poses at 2560x1440 plus frames from the
  scripted flight. Iterate until nothing above "minor" or 5 critic iterations.
- Performance: GPU timestamp queries plus a frame-time log from the scripted fly-through.

DONE WHEN: both gates pass or the iteration cap is reached. Deliver index.html, size, perf report, final critic review.
